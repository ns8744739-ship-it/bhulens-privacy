1. Overview & Privacy Commitment

RudraGrid Technologies (“we”, “our”, or “us”) built BhuLens on a strict local-first, privacy-by-design architecture. Core geospatial features—including satellite map inspection, Uttar Pradesh cadastral parcel overlays, GPS field walk tracking, and manual point-by-point boundary area measurement—work directly on your Android device without requiring mandatory account registration.

What We Never Collect: BhuLens does not collect, request, or store Aadhaar numbers, PAN numbers, bank account details, credit/debit card numbers, contacts, call logs, SMS messages, microphone audio, camera photos, or advertising identifiers (AAID). BhuLens contains zero third-party advertising SDKs (no AdMob or ad trackers) and zero third-party behavioural analytics or crash-tracking SDKs (no Firebase Analytics or Firebase Crashlytics).

3. Information Stored Locally on Your Device vs. Transmitted Over HTTPS

3.1 Strictly Local On-Device Data (Never Uploaded Automatically)

The following data is stored exclusively inside your device’s private application sandbox:

  • Local SQLite Database (khetrekha.db): Saved revenue village bookmarks (villages table), cadastral overlay alignment parameters (cadastral_overlays table), and your user-traced manual boundary polygons, vertices, and area calculations (boundary_measurements table).
  • Map & WMS Tile Cache: Temporarily cached cadastral raster tiles and composite overlays stored in the app’s private cache directory for fast offline/low-connectivity field viewing.
  • Encrypted Local Preferences (EncryptedSharedPreferences): Language selection (English / Hindi), last map camera viewport, and—if you opt into signing in—AES-256-GCM / AES-256-SIV hardware-backed encrypted session tokens and signed cryptographic entitlement leases.
  • Cloud Backup Exclusion: BhuLens explicitly configures android:allowBackup="false" and data_extraction_rules.xml exclusions so your local database and encrypted preferences are not silently extracted to unencrypted cloud backups.

3.2 Data Transmitted Over Encrypted HTTPS Connections

  • Cadastral & Plot Queries to Official Public Portals: When you select a District, Tehsil, Village, or tap a plot number (Gata/Khasra), your device sends the selected administrative/plot code directly over HTTPS to https://upbhunaksha.gov.in to render the cadastral map layer and public Khatauni plot summary. Your personal identity is not attached to these requests.
  • Optional Google Sign-In & Account Management: If you choose to sign in (using Android Credential Manager / Sign in with Google) to manage a BhuLens PRO subscription or submit a Beta bug report, our backend receives your Google-verified basic profile (email address, display name, profile photo URL, and Google subject identifier) and a randomly generated installation UUID (deviceInstallId) to bind your session and cryptographic entitlement lease.
  • Optional Google Play Billing Verification: When you purchase a BhuLens PRO plan, Google Play processes the payment directly. Our backend receives only the Google Play purchaseToken, productId, and package name to verify entitlement status with the Google Play Developer API and issue a cryptographically signed ECDSA P-256 entitlement lease. We never see or store your payment card, UPI ID, or banking credentials.
  • User-Initiated Beta Bug Reports: Only if you are an enrolled Beta tester and explicitly submit a bug report, the app transmits your written bug description and a sanitized diagnostic snapshot (automatically scrubbed by SensitiveDataSanitizer to redact API keys, bearer tokens, JWTs, email addresses, and phone numbers).

5. Data Security Practices

  • HTTPS / TLS Enforcement: Production builds enforce cleartextTrafficPermitted="false" via Android’s Network Security Configuration (network_security_config.xml), blocking unencrypted HTTP traffic.
  • Hardware-Backed Token Encryption: Authentication tokens and signed entitlement leases are encrypted at rest using Android Keystore master keys (androidx.security:security-crypto).
  • Release Log Stripping & PII Redaction: Production release builds use R8/ProGuard rules (-assumenosideeffects on android.util.Log and SafeLog) to strip debug/verbose/info logs, and apply automated regex scrubbing (SensitiveDataSanitizer) to prevent accidental leakage of tokens, keys, or personal identifiers.

6. Data Retention & Your Deletion Rights

  • Deleting Local Data: You can delete individual saved villages, cadastral overlays, or manual boundary measurements at any time directly inside the BhuLens app, or clear all local data immediately by clearing app storage or uninstalling BhuLens from your device.
  • In-App Account Deletion (For Signed-In Users): If you have created an account using Google Sign-In, you can permanently delete your account directly inside the app via Profile & Account → Delete Account. Confirming account deletion immediately revokes all active sessions and entitlement leases, anonymizes and scrubs personal identifiers (email, display name, photo URL, and Google identity binding) from our backend database, and clears local credentials on your device.
